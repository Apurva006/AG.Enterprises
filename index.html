Our Services

Everything your campus
green space needs

01 🎨

Landscape Design & Presentation

Complete design for outdoor spaces — layout plans, plant selection, hardscape and automatic irrigation. Presented with detailed drawings before any work begins.

02 📋

BOQ & Quotation Preparation

After design approval, we prepare complete Bills of Quantities — soil, fertilisers, planting material, vermi compost, sprinklers, pipes and pump systems.

03 ⚙️

Site Execution

Ground levelling, irrigation installation, pre-planting preparation and full plantation — supervised by B.Sc. Agri and B.Tech Agri Engg. qualified experts on site daily.

04 🏏

Cricket Ground Construction

Natural grass pitch and outfield from scratch — turf selection, drainage engineering, clay pitch preparation, rolling, and full annual maintenance on AMC basis.

05 💧

Irrigation Systems

Automatic drip and sprinkler systems designed and installed by B.Tech (Agri Engg.) engineers — optimised for Pune's monsoon-and-dry climate cycle.

06 🔧

Annual Maintenance (AMC)

Scheduled maintenance visits, seasonal treatment, foliar sprays, gap filling and irrigation servicing — keeping your landscape audit-ready all year.

07 👷

Labour Contracting

Reliable contract labour supply for industrial and commercial facilities across the Chakan-Pune MIDC corridor — trained, documented and ready to deploy.

08 🏢

Housekeeping Services

Professional facility housekeeping for factories, industrial parks and office campuses — trained staff, regular schedules, supervisory oversight included.

Founded by Mr. Goraksh Ramchandra Jadhav, the firm has grown from a local landscaping outfit into a preferred vendor for multinational manufacturers, with a D&B verified profile and a client list that includes Volkswagen, Bajaj Auto, Skoda and Thyssenkrupp.
